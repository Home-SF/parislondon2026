Anish Kapoor — Hayward Gallery

Hayward Gallery, Southbank Centre, Belvedere Road, London SE1 8XX, United Kingdom
Tue–Sun, exhibition runs 16 Jun–18 Oct 2026 · Closed Monday · Check website for exact daily hours and late-night openings
£22 adult
Did you know? This major solo show — Kapoor's first full return to the Hayward since his landmark 1998 UK survey — fills the entire gallery building, including Vantablack works, monumental mirror sculptures, and immersive voids, curated by outgoing Hayward director Ralph Rugoff as part of the Southbank Centre's 75th anniversary.
Well known The Hayward Gallery is a leading contemporary arts venue on London's South Bank, opened in 1968 as part of the Brutalist Southbank Centre — one of the finest examples of Brutalist architecture in Europe.

Borough Market

Did you know? One of London’s oldest and most celebrated food markets, Borough Market has traded on this site for over a thousand years. Well known One of London's oldest food markets, Borough Market has existed in some form since at least the 11th century, making it over 1,000 years old. It supplies many of London's top restaurants.

Green Park

Green Park, London SW1A 1BW, UK
Open 24 hours · Free
Free
Did you know? One of the Royal Parks of London, Green Park sits between Hyde Park and St. James’s Park. Unlike most royal parks it has no formal flower beds — just sweeping meadows of grass and mature trees, with a peacefulness that makes it a favourite shortcut between Buckingham Palace and Piccadilly.
Well known The smallest of the central Royal Parks, Green Park is notable for having no formal flower beds or fountains — just mature trees and open grassland, giving it a distinctly peaceful character.
Well known The oldest of London's Royal Parks, St. James's Park was originally a marshy field drained and turned into a deer park by Henry VIII in 1532. Its pelicans have been a feature since 1664.

St. Paul’s Cathedral

St. Paul’s Churchyard, London EC4M 8AD, United Kingdom
Sightseeing Mon–Sat, doors 8:30am (Wed 10am), last entry 4:00pm · Sun open for worship only, no sightseeing · Evensong 5:00pm weekdays, 3:15pm Sun
£27 adult · £10.50 child · free for disabled visitors + one companion
Did you know? Sir Christopher Wren designed the current cathedral after the Great Fire of London destroyed its medieval predecessor in 1666 — its dome, one of the largest in the world, tops out 528 steps up at the Golden Gallery, and the Whispering Gallery lets a whisper against one wall be heard clearly on the opposite side, 30 metres away.
Well known St. Paul’s has hosted major state occasions including Churchill’s funeral and the wedding of Prince Charles and Lady Diana, and its crypt holds the tombs of Wren, Nelson, and the Duke of Wellington.

The View from The Shard

32 London Bridge Street, London SE1 9SG, United Kingdom

Tickets purchased â no changes or cancellations · Ticket includes complimentary digital photos
Entrance Enter via the main lobby at 32 London Bridge Street â the ticket desk and high-speed lifts are on the ground floor. Nearest station: London Bridge (Jubilee & Northern lines).
Did you know? At 309.6 metres, The Shard is the tallest building in the United Kingdom. Designed by Renzo Piano, its viewing galleries on floors 68–72 offer 360° views stretching up to 40 miles on a clear day â you can see five counties, the Thames snaking through London, and landmarks from St Paul’s to Canary Wharf.
Well known At 309.6 metres, The Shard is the tallest building in the United Kingdom, designed by Renzo Piano and completed in 2012. Its 95 habitable floors include offices, restaurants, a hotel, and the viewing gallery.
