9:30 AM
The Louvre
Party of 3 · 3 hours · Tickets already purchased — no changes or cancellations · Nearest métro: Palais Royal – Musée du Louvre (lines 1, 7)

Arrival: Arrive by 9:15 AM and look for a staff member holding a “9:30 AM” sign. Enter through the Porte des Lions entrance. If no one is at that entrance, head straight down into the underground Carrousel du Louvre entrance instead — accessible via the street at 99 Rue de Rivoli or directly from the Palais Royal–Musée du Louvre métro station.

Step-by-Step Route to the Mona Lisa:
1. Clear Security & Enter — Walk through the Porte des Lions gate and pass through the security checkpoint.
2. Take the Stairs — Immediately after scanning your ticket, take the stairs or elevator up to the 1st Floor (Level 1 in Europe) of the Denon Wing.
3. Walk Through the Spanish Galleries — You will emerge into the Spanish and Italian painting galleries. Walk straight down the long corridor (Grand Galerie), moving eastward toward the centre of the museum.
4. Turn Right at Room 711 — Look for the massive wide entrance on your right-hand side leading into Room 711 (Salle des États).
5. Behold the Mona Lisa — She is located on the large freestanding wall directly across from Véronèse’s massive painting, The Wedding Feast at Cana.
